About GemStocks

Run by a collector, not a warehouse.

GemStocks TCG is an independent Australian card seller. The range covers Pokémon and One Piece singles, PSA-graded slabs, sealed boxes and display cases, with a little Lorcana in the mix.

Every listing is photographed in hand, described honestly, and packed and posted by the owner. It is the same approach that has earned five-star ratings on Facebook Marketplace and eBay, now with a simpler way to buy direct.
